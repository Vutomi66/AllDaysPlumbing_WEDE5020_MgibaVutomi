# 1.Overview
### 1.1	Name
# All Days Plumbing
### 1.2	Brief History
All Days Plumbing is a plumbing business in Braamfontein, Johannesburg. It has been around for a while and is known in the city center and around Gauteng for being open 24 hours. People call them when they need regular plumbing work or when there is an emergency.
Because they are in the middle of Braamfontein, they can easily reach many places in Johannesburg. They help homeowners, students, small shops, and other businesses in the area.
It is a small business run by the owner. Most of their customers find them through word-of-mouth and by seeing them locally. They don’t really use the internet to market themselves yet. So making a proper website could really help them grow.
### 1.3	Mission Statement
To provide fast, honest, and reliable plumbing solutions to homes and businesses across Johannesburg, any day of the week.
### 1.4	Vision Statement
To be the most trusted and responsive plumbing service in Braamfontein and greater Johannesburg, known for showing up when it matters most.
### 1.5	Target Audience
•	Homeowners and tenants in Braamfontein, Johannesburg, and nearby areas who have leaks, blocked drains, or broken taps and toilets.
•	Small and medium businesses  like offices, shops, and student housing. They need regular plumbing checks and someone to call in an emergency.
•	Body corporates and landlords who manage lots of flats or houses and need a plumber they can call anytime.
•	Homeowners doing renovations who need new pipes put in or old taps, sinks, and toilets replaced.

# 2.	Website Goals
### 2.1	Goals
The goal of the website is to help All Days Plumbing get more emergency jobs by making it easy for people to call, WhatsApp, or send a message right away. It should also build trust by showing what services they offer, that they are professional, and that they are available 24/7. The website will make it easier for people to find them instead of only hearing about them through word-of-mouth. It will also explain their services clearly, from small repairs to full pipe installations, so customers know what they do before they contact them.

Mobile usability	100% pages mobile-friendly	Manual cross-device testing
# 3.	Current Website Analysis
All Days Plumbing does not have its own website. The only place you can find them online is through a basic Google Business listing. Most of their customers hear about them from friends or family. Their phone number is the only way people can contact them.
### 3.1	Strengths of current presence
All Days Plumbing has a few big strengths. People already know their phone number, so it’s easy to remember. They are also open 24 hours, which is rare for plumbers and makes them different from others. Plus, being in central Braamfontein means they can quickly get to customers all around Johannesburg.
### 3.2	Weaknesses of current presence
Because All Days Plumbing doesn’t have a website, customers can’t check what they do, see prices, or know if they are trustworthy before calling. There are also no photos or reviews to show past work. The only way to contact them is by phone, so people who like WhatsApp or online forms are left out. And since they’re not online, it’s hard for new customers to find them.
# 4.	Proposed website features
### 4.1	HomePage
 The top part of the website should be bold and clear. It must say the business is available 24/7 for emergencies. There should be big Call and WhatsApp buttons so customers can reach them quickly. It should also have a short intro about All Days Plumbing and a quick list of what they do.
### 4.2	About us
Company background, mission and vision, and what sets All Days Plumbing apart (reliability, availability, honest pricing).
### 4.3	Services
•	Finding and fixing leaks – We can find where water is leaking and repair it.
•	Installing and replacing pipes – New pipe work or changing old pipes.
•	Fitting and resealing taps, sinks, and toilets – Putting in new fixtures and making sure they don’t leak.
•	Unblocking drains and pipes – Clearing blocked drains and drainage problems.
•	Emergency and after-hours help – Available 24/7 for urgent plumbing problems.
### 4.4	Enquiry Page
A simple enquiry form allowing visitors to describe their plumbing issue, request a quote, or book a call-out, alongside direct call and WhatsApp links for urgent matters.
### 4.5 Contact Page
Contact details, an map showing the Braamfontein location plus a second representative service area location, operating hours, and a contact form.
# 5. Design and How the Website Works*
### 5.1 Colors 
I will use navy blue and bright orange. Navy blue shows that the business is professional, trustworthy, and reliable. Orange will be used for buttons like “Call” and “WhatsApp” because it shows urgency and that they are ready to help 24 hours.
### 5.2 Fonts
Headings will use a bold, clean font so people can read it quickly. The main text will use a simple, easy-to-read font. This is important because people visiting the site might be stressed during a plumbing emergency.
### 5.3 Layout and Design 
The website will be made for phones first. It will be one column so it’s easy to scroll. There will be a fixed Call/WhatsApp button at the bottom or top so people can contact them anytime. We will also use lots of white space and clear sections so it’s easy to use even when someone is in a hurry.
### 5.4 User Experience
•	Menu : Simple menu with 5 items, and it will look the same on every page.  
•	Mobile friendly : Most people will search for plumbers on their phones, so the site must work well on mobile.  
•	Colors : Navy and orange will make important buttons stand out.  
•	Easy to use : Big buttons for calling or messaging. People shouldn’t have to type a lot.
5.5 Basic Homepage Layout – Wireframe  
•	Top : Logo and menu  
•	Big banner : Headline saying “24/7 Plumbing” + big Call/WhatsApp button  
•	Middle : 3 boxes showing main services  
•	Then : A section with customer reviews  
•	Bottom : Footer with contact details and social media links
